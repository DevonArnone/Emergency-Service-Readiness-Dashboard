# Design System: Aegis Command

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Operator's Workstation"**

Aegis Command looks and behaves like operator software in the spirit of Palantir Gotham: graphite chrome, dense and precise panels, records named by their object type, and a map that is the centerpiece of the product rather than an illustration. It is dark by default; a light theme keeps the same structure with light workspaces. The rail and every stage (maps, timelines, the relationship graph, charts) stay graphite in both themes.

Each page leads with one primary workspace, and detail arrives on selection. Selecting a record in a row, on the map, on a timeline, or in the graph moves one cobalt selection through every linked view and opens the inspector beside the work.

**Key characteristics**

- Graphite neutrals, 1px hairlines, and one accent, cobalt, for selection, focus, and the primary action.
- Tight corners: 4px controls, 6px panels, 8px stages, 3px tags and badges.
- Uppercase micro-labels for metadata (summary labels, table heads, inspector sections); sentence case for everything a person reads as language.
- Records named the same way everywhere: an object chip (icon plus `INCIDENT`, `APPARATUS`, `PERSON`, `STATION`, `SHIFT`, `CREDENTIAL`, `ALERT`) followed by a mono identifier.
- Depth that carries information: fly-to on selection, halos on what is selected, arcs for real assignments, columns for real counts, a hillshaded basemap, and a dimmed world outside the county.

**The Meaningful Depth Rule.** Every glow, arc, column, and motion encodes something recorded. Nothing decorative moves, and nothing implies a prediction the data cannot support: station areas are nearest-station by straight line, and rings are straight-line miles.

## Colors

All values are defined once in `dashboard/app/styles/tokens.css` and mirrored in the frontmatter (`colors` for light, `colors-dark` for the dark overrides).

- **Rail** (`navy-*`, graphite despite the name): navigation rail, tooltips, sign-in background.
- **Workspace** (`bg`, `surface`, `surface-2`, `surface-sunken`, `ink`, `ink-2`, `ink-3`, `rule`, `rule-strong`): panels, tables, forms, and the inspector.
- **Cobalt** (`cobalt`, `cobalt-strong`, `cobalt-soft`, `cobalt-rule`, `cobalt-ink`): selection, focus, the primary action, links.
- **State** (`ok`, `warn`, `bad` with `-soft`, `-rule`, `-ink`): always paired with a word. `bad-solid` fills count bubbles in both themes.
- **Stage** (`stage`, `stage-2`, `stage-3`, `stage-rule`, `stage-ink`, `stage-ink-2`, `stage-ok`, `stage-warn`, `stage-bad`, `stage-cobalt`, `stage-glow`): the luminous palette for marks on graphite. Timeline fills (`stage-assigned`, `stage-oos`, `stage-maintenance`, the `stage-hatch-*` pair) and duty fills (`duty-*`) are tokens too.
- **Map** (`map-*`): the basemap palette. The MapLibre style reads these tokens at runtime, so the basemap, the SVG fallback, and the rest of the stage share one source.

**The One Accent Rule.** Cobalt means "selected", "focused", or "press this". The informational tone borrows its family; nothing else does.

**The Word Plus Color Rule.** State is a word plus a color. Map marks have legend entries, timeline bars carry stage names, badges carry words.

**The Quiet Until It Matters Rule.** `ratioTone` in `dashboard/lib/utils.ts` tones readiness, staffing, and availability: quiet at 85% and above, warning from 60%, critical below.

**The Two Palettes Rule.** Workspace state colors go on workspace surfaces; `stage-*` colors go on the stage.

## Typography

IBM Plex Sans Variable for interface text, IBM Plex Mono for identifiers, clock times, coordinates, and aligned figures. Both are self-hosted.

- **Headline** 1.75rem / 600: one per page.
- **Figure** 1.625rem / 600, tabular: summary strip values, with the label above and the detail below.
- **Record title** 1.125rem / 600: inspector heading.
- **Title** 0.9375rem / 600: panel and stage headings.
- **Body** 0.9375rem; **Control** 0.875rem / 500; table cells 0.875rem.
- **Micro-label** 0.6875rem / 600, uppercase, 0.08em tracking: summary labels, table column heads, inspector section heads, object chips, layer panel heading.
- **Mono**: unit and incident IDs, station numbers, times, coordinates, ruler ticks.

**The Mono Is Data Rule.** Mono never sets a heading or a sentence.

**The Labels Are Metadata Rule.** Uppercase tracking is only for short labels that name a field or a column. Headings, buttons, and sentences stay sentence case.

## Layout

A fixed graphite rail (240px) and a sticky top bar over one scrolling page with 20px block gaps. Reading order: page header, summary strip, toolbar or view switch, primary workspace.

- **County overview is map-first.** The map spans the full width at roughly the viewport height. From 1280px the attention queue (or the selected station) docks over the map's right edge and the layer panel over its left; the map's controls, attribution, and framing keep clear of the dock. The 24-hour activity scrubber sits directly beneath the map. Resource posture and the handover brief follow below.
- **Inspector.** Docks at 400px from 1280px; below that it is a full-height drawer that returns focus when closed.
- **Phones.** Summary figures form a two-column grid (all visible, reading order). Tables keep only the columns that fit and never scroll sideways on the overview. Touch targets reach 44px. The theme toggle moves into command search below 420px.

**The One Workspace Rule** and **The Selection Travels Rule** hold: alternative views (register, map, timeline, graph) replace each other behind a segmented switch, and the URL carries the selection.

## Elevation & Depth

- **Panel**: hairline border plus a faint shadow.
- **Raised**: stages and the docked inspector.
- **Float**: panels docked over the map and graph (`shadow-float`), opaque graphite with a 1px rule. A light backdrop blur is allowed only over the map.
- **Overlay**: dialogs, drawers, menus, command search.
- **Glow** (`stage-glow`): reserved for the selected thing and the "now" playheads. Never on hover.

## Shapes

4px controls, 6px panels, 8px stages, 3px tags (badges, object chips, count bubbles, access tag). Map marks: a station is a disc (ring when normal, filled amber or red when it needs attention); an incident is a diamond with a mark, pulsing while active; a readiness column is a hexagonal prism. A diagonal hatch means unrecorded, untimed, cancelled, or below-minimum time.

## Components

- **Buttons and fields**: 34px (44px below 768px). Primary is cobalt; secondary is bordered; danger is outlined red; ghost is cobalt ink.
- **Status badge**: square-cornered tag with a dot and a word.
- **Object chip**: type icon plus uppercase type, then a mono identifier. Leads every inspector header.
- **Summary strip**: one bordered strip of labeled figures; the readiness cell carries a 14-day sparkline from the trends endpoint.
- **Inspector (object view)**: object chip, record title, kind line, state badge; facts render as a bordered property sheet; sections use micro-label heads; actions live in the footer. The incident inspector includes a compact one-hop relationship graph.
- **County map (`GeoMap`)**: MapLibre GL on OpenFreeMap vector tiles with a custom graphite style, AWS Terrain Tiles hillshade, 3D buildings from zoom 13, a mask that dims everything outside the county, and a glowing boundary. deck.gl overlays response arcs (home station to incident, colored by type), nearest-station areas, risk halos, 1/3/5-mile rings around the selected station, and, in 3D, hexagonal readiness columns sized by assigned units and scaled with zoom. Station and incident markers are DOM buttons (`data-map-station`, `data-map-incident`) so they are focusable and testable. Tools stack at the right (zoom, reset, 3D, compass, fullscreen); a coordinate readout and scale bar sit bottom-left; the layer panel lists every layer with counts. Without WebGL, `MapView` falls back to the SVG `CountyMap`.
- **Activity scrubber**: a 24-hour histogram of incident openings and alerts in 30-minute bins with a "now" playhead. Drag (or arrow keys) to choose a window; the map and queue filter to it and a removable chip names it.
- **Relationship graph (`LinkGraph`)**: a force layout of incidents (diamonds), apparatus (rectangles), stations (hexagons), and crew (dots), built only from recorded assignments. Hover highlights one hop; click selects; drag pans or pins; scroll zooms. An equivalent list sits behind it.
- **Timelines**: incident lifecycle, unit deployment history, and the duty roster share one grammar: sticky mono ruler, faint grid, a glowing cobalt "now" line, square-cornered bars built from recorded facts, hatched unknown time, a hover crosshair with a time readout, and a cobalt glow on the selected row. The duty roster groups apparatus under their home station and shades the elapsed part of each window.
- **States**: empty, loading, error, and notice share one vocabulary across the app.

## Motion

One decelerating curve (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`). Hover changes color only. Selection flies the map to the record over 1.4s; 3D tilts over 1.1s. Active incidents pulse, the live connection dot beacons, and the sync time flashes once per snapshot. Summary figures count up once on first load, never on realtime refetch. Under reduced motion every animation collapses and pulses become static rings.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do

- Lead with the map on the overview and keep docked panels opaque and clear of the map's controls.
- Name records with an object chip and a mono identifier.
- Encode only recorded data in depth: arcs for assignments, columns for counts, rings for straight-line distance.
- Pair every state color with a word and keep healthy figures quiet.
- Keep write controls visible but disabled with a reason in read-only sessions; confirm destructive actions.

### Don't

- Don't add neon, fake HUD ornament, or glow on hover.
- Don't imply drive time, response coverage, or prediction.
- Don't let a table scroll sideways inside a summary card; drop or merge columns instead.
- Don't add a second accent hue or use cobalt as decoration.
- Don't show register, map, timeline, and graph of the same records at once.
